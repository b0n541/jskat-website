The game

Classic rules, ready when you are

JSkat follows the shape of a familiar Skat round while giving you space to practice and experiment.

  • Play suit, grand, and null games
  • Bid through a complete three-player round
  • Pass in a game when nobody bids
  • Calculate wins and losses after every game
  • Play according to official Skat rules
  • Explore pub-rule variants as they are added

Computer opponents

Different minds across the table

Choose an opponent that fits the kind of game you want to play. Version 0.24 adds JSkat’s first capable machine learning player and keeps simpler opponents available for comparison and testing.

  • Machine learning player A new opponent in 0.24 that learns a richer strategy from game data.
  • Algorithmic player Uses careful bidding and rule-based decisions for declarer and defenders.
  • Mr. Random Makes random choices and remains useful for testing unexpected game situations.
