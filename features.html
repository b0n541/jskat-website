What JSkat can do

Everything you need to play Skat

Play the usual Skat games against several computer players. You can also open every hand or review completed tricks when you want to understand a game in more detail.

Rules and game types

Play by the official Skat rules

JSkat handles the bidding, game rules, and scoring while you decide how to play your cards.

  • Play suit, grand, and null games
  • Bid through a complete three-player round
  • Pass in a game when nobody bids
  • Calculate wins and losses after every game
  • Play according to official Skat rules
  • Explore pub-rule variants as they are added

Computer opponents

Choose your opponents

JSkat includes several computer players with different approaches. Version 0.24 adds the first one based on machine learning, while the simpler players remain useful for testing and comparison.

  • Machine learning player New in version 0.24 and trained on game data.
  • Algorithmic player Uses fixed rules for bidding and for playing as declarer or defender.
  • Mr. Random Makes random choices and remains useful for testing unexpected game situations.
